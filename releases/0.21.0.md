# Epizen 0.21.0

Calendar gives the schedule more room. Click the underlined logged time to see the day's or week's category breakdown, productive and recovery time, completed tasks, and Apple Calendar event count. The weekly breakdown can open the existing weekly note.

Click the clock above the time grid to set when your day starts and ends. Week and Day share the saved range. Click a folded band to see the hours outside it, or right-click the band to edit that boundary. Baskerville date headings and tabular SF Pro times give the schedule a clearer hierarchy.

Daily notes created from the calendar can now use the folder and filename pattern from Obsidian Daily Notes. An explicit Epizen naming choice takes precedence. Existing notes remain readable in their original locations. Checking a habit keeps it in place and keeps habit Markdown out of the CBT stream. Weekly and monthly sleep entries and averages read in hours and minutes.

Use **⇧⌘R** to reveal the current file and **⇧⌘F** to search filenames and note content. The File menu also opens recent files, shows the explorer, and creates folders. All five shortcuts are configurable in **Settings → Hotkeys**.

For Apple Silicon Macs. The app is ad-hoc signed and ships without Developer ID notarization.

Coverage includes habit and thought-record edits on demo-vault copies, mixed daily-note date formats, shortcut configuration, and calendar hours and activity. Wide and narrow calendar previews were inspected with a simulated vault and events. GitHub’s macOS checks use the same demo fixtures and a simulated calendar.
