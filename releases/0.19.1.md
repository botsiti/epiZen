# Epizen 0.19.1

Week and Day now fill the space below the calendar toolbar. On a large screen, the extra height goes to the hour rows. In a short window, the schedule scrolls so appointments stay readable.

Short appointments keep a title and a separate click target, even when two fall just minutes apart. The date, navigation, view switch, and timer share a flatter header.

Existing notes and calendar events require no migration. This build is for Apple Silicon Macs and uses ad-hoc signing. Developer ID notarization is unavailable.

Headless layout checks covered the full-height week and day, narrow-window scrolling, and drawing a half-hour event after resizing.

All seven fast checks passed. The production frontend also passed an offscreen WebKit check using a demo-vault copy and a simulated calendar. The grid reached the pane bottom at 1920 × 1040 and 1280 × 820. The native app window check is deferred because Desktop 2 is unavailable on the built-in display.
