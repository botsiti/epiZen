# Epizen 0.15.0

Habits now supports daily checkboxes, targets such as eight glasses of water, and measurements such as hours of sleep. Select a habit to see its current and best streak, completion rate, activity calendar and next milestone. Goals show daily progress bars. Measurements show a trend with gaps on days you didn’t log.

- **Make a routine your own.** Choose a color and icon, check in from Habits or the daily note, and keep the same history when you archive or reactivate a habit.
- **Take your history with you.** Export the habit journal as Markdown or copy it to share. The journal includes active, archived and historical routines, their schedules, dated entries, and the source definitions. The Files sidebar hides the top-level Habits and Templates folders. Their files stay in your vault.
- **Choose a theme.** Open Settings → Appearance to preview 24 palettes, including Gruvbox and Catppuccin Mocha, or paste a custom palette. The workspace and its title bar have an opaque backing, including when Liquid Glass is enabled.
- **Move and dictate to Spike.** Drag the companion to place him on the screen. On macOS 26 and later, Spike can transcribe on this Mac with Keyboard Dictation turned off when Apple supports the selected language. The first use may download a language asset. You can dismiss a recording error and return to typing.

Existing checkbox habits work without migration. Numeric habits use ordinary Markdown frontmatter and inline fields. The required PR check and full desktop workflow record validation for this release.

For macOS on Apple Silicon. The app uses an ad-hoc signature and ships without Apple Developer ID notarization. To roll back, reinstall Epizen 0.14.0 and keep your vault. That version displays daily checkboxes and leaves the newer measurement fields in Markdown.
